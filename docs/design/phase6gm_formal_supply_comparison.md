# Phase 6GM formal supply comparison

## Frozen pre-runtime boundary

Phase 6GL remains a nonreplaceable public-channel operation safe stop. Its
attempt01 and every earlier Phase 6FO/6GA--6GK artifact remain read-only and are
not members of Phase 6GM. Phase 6GM starts from an empty artifact root under the
machine contract `scripts/phase6gm_supply_comparison_contract.json` (SHA-256
`D1DFA1B592955A2EFC3BA88A79D34C37E9040DE6CE859CE068A0DA0369CF7D54`).

The corrected-four geometry, Point policies and float32 payload contract, Flow
physics, CollisionProxy Mesh, transforms, frames, authored-Mesh spatial bands,
directional transport proxy, numerical gates, balanced population, and safety
limits are copied unchanged from Phase 6GL. Production stage construction and
defaults are outside this Phase.

## Shared diagnostic export boundary

The former drift was caused by separate control-path authoring: Phase 6GK used
the Phase 6GD divergence control, while Phase 6GL used the base stage without
that switch. Phase 6GM replaces the duplicated setting with one immutable
diagnostic descriptor and helper:

- `scripts/phase6gm_flow_export_state_descriptor.json`
- `scripts/phase6gm_flow_export_state.py`

The descriptor is derived from the qualified Phase 6GK schema evidence and has
semantic digest
`53CDE38FD5B1A5F48AB2E7B896F6EF391DDA4D5F6621B21FDB1B435F34BDA8CE`.
It explicitly enables temperature, fuel, burn, smoke, velocity, and divergence,
and explicitly disables RGBA and RGB. Enabled/readback/statistics are also
explicit. The formal wrapper and the offline fixture consume this same
descriptor; the frozen Phase 6GK wrapper and production builder are not edited.

Before Kit can launch, the offline fixture writes and reopens actual USD files
for S93, S100, and Collision OFF. It requires the qualified Prim path/type,
explicit root-layer bool opinions, identical export and corrected-four geometry
digests, expected Point payload differences, identical S100/OFF payload, and
only the intended Collision switch. Missing, wrong-Prim, wrong-type, duplicate,
and inherited-only negative fixtures must fail closed. The fixture uses local
USD Python and records that no Kit process was launched.

## Runtime and stopping rule

The required population is three independent processes for S93, S100, and OFF
in the frozen balanced order, with at most two startup-prerequisite replacements
and at most eleven launches. Each accepted process performs exactly three
public readbacks at frames 180, 360, and 540. Raw seven-handle schema validation
precedes semantics; ordered alias release, canonical bounded-artifact property,
and zero ownership residual remain mandatory.

Kit/tree limits remain 16/17 GiB, runner/diagnostic limits 512 MiB, physical and
commit floors 8 GiB, and stage close 180 seconds. Release-after-close, durable
pre-close commit, progress-aware stack-first CDB, exact attempt-tree cleanup,
and PID-reuse classification are inherited unchanged. Any non-startup failure
stops the population without retry. Video is authorized only after numerical
9/9 qualification, and production integration remains out of scope.
